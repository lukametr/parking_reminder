# Privacy Policy for SOS App

Last updated: 28 August 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how SOS App ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

SOS App provides quick access to parking-related actions and helpful shortcuts. The App is designed to operate without creating user accounts and without collecting personally identifiable information.

## Summary
- We do not sell or rent your data.
- We do not require an account or login.
- We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data.
- Limited technical information may be processed to make the App work (for example, crash logs and basic diagnostics) as described below.

## Information We Collect
We aim to collect as little data as possible.

1) Information you provide directly
- The App does not require you to submit personal information. If you choose to contact us for support, we may receive your email and the content of your message.

2) Automatically collected information
- Device and app diagnostics: On some platforms, we may receive anonymous crash reports, performance data, and basic device information (e.g., OS version, device model, app version) to improve stability and performance.
- Network requests: When you tap an action that opens your phone dialer, SMS app, or a web link, those actions are handled by your device or the external service; we do not receive or store the content of those communications.

We do not collect the following data categories: precise or approximate location, contacts, calendars, photos, media, files, audio, camera, SMS contents, call logs, or sensitive personal information.

## How We Use Information
- To operate, maintain, and improve the App’s features and performance.
- To troubleshoot issues, diagnose crashes, and ensure compatibility across devices.
- To respond to your support requests (if you contact us).

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your data. We do not share personal data with third parties except:
- Service providers: If we use analytics or crash reporting tools (e.g., Google/Firebase), they will process limited technical data on our behalf under their terms.
- Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or legal process.

## Data Retention
- Crash and performance data (if collected) are retained only as long as needed to analyze and improve the App and in accordance with our service providers’ retention policies.
- Support communications are retained as needed to resolve your inquiry and comply with legal obligations.

## Children’s Privacy
The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Choices
- Uninstall: You can stop all information collection by uninstalling the App.
- Limit diagnostics: You can adjust your device’s system settings to limit sharing of diagnostics and usage data with app developers and platform services.

## Data Security
We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## International Data Transfers
If information is processed by our service providers (e.g., analytics/crash reporting), it may be transferred to and processed on servers outside your country. We rely on appropriate safeguards where required by law.

## Third-Party Services
The App may contain links or actions that open third‑party services (e.g., phone dialer, SMS, or web pages). Your interactions with those services are governed by their privacy policies.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be available at this URL. Material changes will be effective upon posting unless otherwise stated.
